# How to License These Frameworks

1. **Register copyright** — copyright.gov, $65 per work online. Do this before any deal; you need registration to sue for infringement in the US.
2. **Decide the offer** — per-framework price, bundle price, or royalty. See `CATALOG.md` for the five works.
3. **Use the license template** — fill in `LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md` per buyer: who, what scope, how much, how long. One signed copy each.
4. **Sell it** — Gumroad or your own site + Stripe. Attach the signed license to every delivery.
5. **Track it** — keep a simple log: buyer, work, date, amount, term. A spreadsheet is fine.
6. **Enforce it** — if someone uses your work unpaid, the registration + signed licenses are your leverage. Lawyer up before sending threats.

Start with registration. Everything else is paperwork after that.
